# Infrastructure Access

Checkpoint-based evaluation is configured for GPUs with approximately
24 GB of GPU memory. This capacity may not be consistently available
in common public environments such as standard Google Colab sessions.

Evaluators may use any compatible infrastructure. As an optional
fallback, the authors can provide temporary SSH access to an isolated
evaluation server.

Temporary access credentials will be provided only through ACSAC
HotCRP and will remain valid during the artifact evaluation period.
